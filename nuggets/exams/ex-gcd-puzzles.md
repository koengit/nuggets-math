(a) For positive integers $n$, $m$ we know that $\gcd(n, m)$ is odd and that $7n - 9m = 10$. What values can $\gcd(n, m)$ have?

(b) For a positive integer $a$ we know that $(a + 1) \mid (a^2 + 1)$. What values can $a$ have?

(c) $p$, $p + 2$ and $p + 4$ are all prime. What can $p$ be? Give all possibilities and show that there are no others. *Hint:* look at what happens modulo 3.

## Hint

(a) Any common divisor of $n$ and $m$ divides $7n - 9m$. (b) Write $a^2 + 1 = (a+1)(a-1) + 2$. (c) Exactly one of three numbers with remainders $p, p+2, p+4$ modulo 3 is divisible by 3.
