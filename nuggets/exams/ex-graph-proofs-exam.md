(a) $G$ is a simple graph with $n$ nodes and no cycles. How many edges can $G$ have at most? Prove your claim. (You may use results proved in the course; state clearly what they say.)

(b) $G$ is a simple graph with **exactly one** node $u$ of degree 1. Show that $G$ contains a cycle. *Hint:* consider the longest path starting in $u$.

## Hint

(a) What does each connected component look like? Use [[tree-edges]]. (b) Where can the last node of a longest path from $u$ have its other neighbours?
