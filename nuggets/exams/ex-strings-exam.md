You may give your answers as expressions with numbers, $+, -, \cdot, /$, factorials and $\binom nk$; no need to compute them.

(a) How many different strings can be made from the 9 letters K, A, L, L, E, A, N, K, A (each used exactly once)?
(b) In how many of these are the two L's next to each other?
(c) In how many ways can the letters of MATEMATIK be arranged so that no vowel directly follows another vowel? (Example: METAMATIK is fine, TAAMEMKIT is not.) *Hint:* place the consonants M, T, M, T, K first, then the vowels A, E, A, I. Note that M, T and A occur twice.

## Hint

See [[words-with-repeated-letters]] for all three techniques: dividing by repetitions, gluing letters together, and placing letters in gaps.
