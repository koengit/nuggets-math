Find all integers $x$ satisfying both congruences (and, for (a) and (b), all such $x$ between 0 and 200). Use the Pulverizer and show all steps.

(a) $x \equiv 5 \pmod{11}$ and $x \equiv 6 \pmod 7$
(b) $x \equiv 6 \pmod{11}$ and $x \equiv 5 \pmod 7$
(c) $x \equiv 7 \pmod{11}$ and $x \equiv 5 \pmod 6$
(d) $x \equiv 3 \pmod 7$ and $x \equiv 4 \pmod{11}$
(e) $x \equiv 4 \pmod 8$ and $x \equiv 5 \pmod{11}$

## Hint

See [[chinese-remainder-theorem]] or [[crt-direct-method]]. The solutions form one congruence class modulo the product of the moduli; check your answer against both congruences.
