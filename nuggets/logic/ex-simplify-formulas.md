Give a simpler formula that means the same, and check your answer with a truth table.

(a) $\neg(p \Leftrightarrow q)$  (b) $\neg p \Leftrightarrow \neg q$  (c) $\neg p \Rightarrow \neg q$

## Answer

(a) $\neg p \Leftrightarrow q$ (or equivalently $p \Leftrightarrow \neg q$; it is "exclusive or").

(b) $p \Leftrightarrow q$.

(c) $q \Rightarrow p$ (this is the contrapositive).
