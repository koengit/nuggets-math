There are three cards: one is blue on both sides, one is red on both sides, and one is blue on one side and red on the other. A card is chosen at random and put on the table; the side you see is **red**. What is the probability that the other side is **blue**?

## Answer

$\frac13$ (so the other side is red with probability $\frac23$) — not $\frac12$!

The equally likely outcomes are not the 3 *cards* but the 6 *sides* that could be facing up. Three of them are red: the two sides of the red–red card, and the red side of the red–blue card. Only in the last case is the other side blue. So $P = \frac13$.
