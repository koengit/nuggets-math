A course has 54 students (including you), randomly divided into 9 groups of 6. You are secretly in love with one classmate. What is the probability that you two end up in the same group?

## Answer

Put yourself in a group first. In your group there are 5 spots left, and in total 53 spots left for your classmate, all equally likely. So the probability is
$$ \frac{5}{53} . $$

(Counting all divisions into groups ([[dividing-into-groups]]) gives the same answer, with a lot more work. Choosing the right point of view makes probability problems much easier.)
