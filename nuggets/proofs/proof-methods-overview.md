These are the proof methods we use throughout the course. Each has its own nugget with an example. The Swedish names are given too, since you will meet them in Swedish textbooks.

| Method | Swedish | Nugget |
|---|---|---|
| equational reasoning | likhetsresonemang | [[equational-reasoning]] |
| inequational reasoning | olikhetsresonemang | [[inequational-reasoning]] |
| using a lemma | att använda en hjälpsats | [[using-a-lemma]] |
| case splitting | falluppdelning | [[case-splitting]] |
| proof by contradiction | motsägelsebevis | [[proof-by-contradiction]] |
| simple induction | (enkel) induktion / första induktionsprincipen | [[simple-induction]] |
| strong induction | stark induktion / andra induktionsprincipen | [[strong-induction]] |
| structural induction | strukturell induktion | [[structural-induction]] |
| proof by analogy | analogibevis | — |

A general piece of advice that holds for all of them: a proof is written for a *reader*. Say which method you use, say what you assume, say what you are going to show, and give a reason for every step.
