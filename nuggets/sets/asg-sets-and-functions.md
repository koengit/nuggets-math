*(Assignment questions; no answers are published.)*

**1.** Here are three properties two sets $A$ and $B$ may have:
$$ A \cup B = \{1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7\}, \qquad A \cap B = \{2, 6\}, \qquad A \setminus B = \{1, 4, 7\}. $$
(a) Find sets $A$ and $B$ that satisfy all three at the same time. (b) Is your solution the only one? Explain.

**2.** Find a function $f : \Z \to \N$ that is **injective** but **not surjective**. Explain why your function has / does not have these properties. (Note: the domain is now $\Z$, not $\N$ as in [[ex-injective-not-surjective]].)
